# FreeBSD Foundation CRA Update \- August and September 2026

Note: Due to delays in reporting, August and September reports are combined.

## Summary

During August and September, the Foundation continued to move CRA readiness from planning into implementation. Key internal Foundation readiness documentation has been developed, and public guidance for FreeBSD community members and business users is now available. 

Technical work on the SBOM toolchain continues, with further integration, review and upstream work required. The Foundation also increased community engagement at EuroBSDcon and the FreeBSD DevSummit.

## Workstream updates

### Security, and vulnerability handling

The Foundation Cybersecurity policy, a legal obligation for Open Source Software Stewards under the CRA, has been developed with input from the Core and Security Teams. As it reflects the existing relationship between the FreeBSD Foundation and Project rather than seeking to change it, and as it is primarily intended to demonstrate compliance with CRA legal obligations, the Foundation will retain this as an internal document and provide it to Market Surveillance Authorities on demand.

The CRA Reporting Protocol for the FreeBSD Foundation has also been completed and has been socialized with the FreeBSD Core and Security Teams. It will remain an internal resource for now, as it primarily provides Foundation staff with a practical briefing and checklist for meeting the Foundation’s obligations as an open-source software steward in the case of a live incident.

### SBOM toolchain

Development and testing of the SBOM solution continue, with feedback being addressed as the implementation matures. The proposal to generate SBOMs for the FreeBSD base system is under active review, and there are likely to be minimal additional changes to the base system needed to support the proposed approach. We will continue to seek input from assigned reviewers and potentially the Source Manager Team. Once ready, the intention is for the SBOM solution to be MFC’d to FreeBSD 14 and 15\.

Work is also progressing on the wider SBOM ecosystem. There are plans in the community to propose an SBOM solution for ports, and the Foundation will work with the community members to determine how this relates to the funded work already underway and how best to implement it collaboratively. Separately, work has started in the community to make the ports license format SPDX-compliant, with the transition expected to be completed in Q2 2027\. Some downstream manufacturers have also expressed an interest in supporting the SBOM work because of their own CRA compliance requirements by the end of 2027, and the Foundation is in contact with them to understand how this can be achieved.

Other manufacturers who are also interested should contact the Foundation at [cra@freebsdfoundation.org](mailto:cra@freebsdfoundation.org). 

CycloneDX SBOM support in pkgconf has been developed to reach parity with SPDX 3.0.1. The work is now code-complete in the developer’s local branch. We hope these changes can be included in the upstream pkgconf 3.1 release, potentially later this year.

### Communications

The Foundation ran a day-long [CRA and SBOM tutorial at EuroBSDcon](https://events.eurobsdcon.org/2026/talk/RER8UZ/) in collaboration with Peter Hansteen of BSDly. The session provided an opportunity to explain the CRA, its implications for open-source software and FreeBSD, and the role of SBOMs in supporting compliance.

The Foundation also presented a project update at the FreeBSD DevSummit colocated with EuroBSDcon, titled *EU Cyber Resilience Act \- How it affects FreeBSD and what to do about it*. [Video and slides from the session are available through the Dev Summit wiki.](https://wiki.freebsd.org/DevSummit/202609)
