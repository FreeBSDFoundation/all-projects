## Summary

### Status 

In progress

### **Commentary**

* Work has begun on all of the work packages except one (WP D) which could not be started due to the developer being unavailable until December 2026\. One of the developers assigned to WP C has also not yet started work on the project.  
* As a result, the number of hours completed in October was lower than originally planned.  
* We have begun to set up a public tracking repo for the project so that our community stakeholders can understand what is being done and follow the project’s progress.  
  * https\://github.com/FreeBSDFoundation/all-projects/tree/main/FreeBSD%20Improvements%20for%20Enterprise%20(STA%20commissioned)  
* Each developer is keeping a weekly journal of their progress on the relevant work package. Please follow the links below to read these, they contain information about work completed for each work package. Note: Some of the developers are overdue on providing their updates, we are working on tightening up the process.

### **Risks, concerns and variations**

As mentioned, we have been unable to start WP D and one of the developers assigned to WP C has not yet started work on the project. This resulted in fewer hours being completed and invoiced in October than originally planned. We are reviewing the options and working towards a proposed alternative plan.
